    What I refuse to rush.

    • Intent before parts

      I begin with how the finished object should feel, sound, function and live on your desk — then choose components around that goal.

    • Visible process

      Customers should understand what is being done and why. The craft is part of the experience, not something hidden behind a checkout page.

    • Curated, not crowded

      I would rather present fewer, more considered options than bury the experience under hundreds of near-identical choices.

    • Built to keep

      The goal is an object that still feels deliberate after the novelty wears off — something worth using, maintaining and keeping.
