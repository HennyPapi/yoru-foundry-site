HOW A COMMISSION WORKS

From first conversation to final handoff.

  1. 01

    Request

    Tell me what you know, what you like, and what you want the finished board to do for you.

  2. 02

    Consultation

    We narrow the layout, feel, sound, materials, connectivity and budget without forcing technical jargon on you.

  3. 03

    Parts & Design

    I source or confirm the case, PCB, plate, switches, stabilizers, keycaps and finishing choices.

  4. 04

    Build

    Preparation, assembly and any selected switch or stabilizer work happen at the bench.

  5. 05

    Tuning & QC

    The board is tested for feel, sound, consistency, firmware, connectivity and final presentation.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    You receive the completed build with the final configuration documented for future reference.

PRICING

Quoted around the build.

Every commission is quoted individually. Cost depends on the platform, case and materials, switches, keycaps, tuning work, optional modifications, sourcing and the level of customization involved.

The goal is not to upsell the most expensive parts. It is to put the budget where it changes the finished experience.
