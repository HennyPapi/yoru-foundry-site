C with the cable

A braided USB-C cable runs from behind the medallion to the keys: a straight run, a coiled section with copper collars, another run, then the plug tucked under the first key. The straight runs stretch or shrink with the window; the coil and plug stay the same size.

The same header in a 1,000px window: there is no room for the coil, so the cable becomes one short straight run into the plug.

C with an LED cable

The same cable with beads of warm light travelling through it from left to right, one after another, spiralling through the coil (dimmer where they pass behind a loop) and fading into the plug. Turned off for anyone who has reduced motion set on their device.

A. The bottom row

Read left to right like a real bottom row: the medallion on the Windows-key spot, two modifiers, a blank spacebar, two more modifiers, then Enter for the commission. Crafted Art is the current page, so its key is pressed down. Hover any key; click and hold to press it.

B. Spacebar with the medallion

The medallion sits on the spacebar, like an artisan spacebar on a custom board. The spacebar is the home link.

C. Keys on the right

The medallion stays on its own at the left; the links are a short run of keys on the right, ending in Enter. Closest to today's layout.

On a phone
