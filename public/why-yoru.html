WHY YORU

Small by design.

Yoru Foundry is not trying to win by carrying the most products. The point is direct communication, careful decisions, visible process and a finished object that feels considered.

01

Direct to the builder

The same person discussing the goal is the person working at the bench. Less translation means fewer details get lost.

02

Curated choices

You do not need to know every switch, plate and mounting system. I narrow the choices around what you actually want to feel and hear.

03

Documented process

Preparation, tuning, testing and the final configuration are treated as part of the commission rather than invisible labor.

04

Quality control

A build is not considered finished when it turns on. Feel, consistency, sound, connectivity and presentation all get a final pass.

05

No forced upsell

The most expensive option is not automatically the right option. Budget goes where it meaningfully changes the experience.

06

Built to keep

The goal is to create something that still feels deliberate once the excitement of something new has passed.

THE IDEA

Useful things can still be art.

Craft is not reserved for objects displayed behind glass. It can live in something you touch every day.
