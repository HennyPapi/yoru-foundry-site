THE FOUNDRY

Meet the Founder. Your Builder.

Yoru Foundry is personal by design. The person you speak with is the person thinking through the details, working at the bench, and refining your build.

Founder portrait / workbench photo

MIKE • FOUNDER / BUILDER

I’ve always been drawn to making things better with my own hands.

I grew into keyboards through the same instinct that has always pulled me toward tinkering: take something apart, understand why it feels the way it does, and rebuild it with more intention.

For me, Yoru Foundry is not about making ordinary objects look expensive. It is about caring enough to notice the things most people overlook — proportion, texture, sound, movement, finish, and the small decisions that make an object feel complete.

Keyboards are where the foundry begins, but the idea is larger than keyboards. I want Yoru Foundry to become a home for useful objects made with the kind of care normally reserved for things we call art.

WHAT YORU MEANS

Quiet focus. Depth. Atmosphere.

Yoru evokes night — not darkness for its own sake, but the quieter hours where details become easier to notice.

WHAT FOUNDRY MEANS

Raw pieces, refined through process.

A foundry transforms material through heat, judgment, and work. That process is the metaphor behind the brand.
