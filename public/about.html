THE FOUNDRY

About Yoru Foundry.

Yoru Foundry began with a fascination for the difference between something that merely works and something that feels considered.

Why Yoru Foundry exists.

There is art in objects that are not traditionally called art. A keyboard, a desk accessory, a tool, or an everyday object can still carry proportion, texture, sound, intention, and personality.

The foundry is built around that idea: take useful things seriously enough to refine them.

The meaning of the name.

Yoru evokes night — restraint, depth, atmosphere, and a quieter kind of focus. Foundry speaks to making: raw parts transformed through process, judgment, and craft.

Our standard.

“Refined by Craft.”

Not made to look expensive. Made to feel intentional. The goal is quality you can see, hear, touch, and keep.
