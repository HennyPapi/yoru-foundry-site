THE PROCESS

Intent first. Parts second.

We start with what the object should feel like, how it should serve you, and what kind of presence it should have. The component choices follow from that intention.

01

Define the foundation

Layout, material, structure, connectivity, use case, and budget.

02

Shape the experience

Feel, feedback, ergonomics, and the details you interact with every day.

03

Tune the character

Sound, finish, balance, and the small choices that give a build its identity.

04

Refine the presentation

Color, accents, materials, and final assembly brought together with restraint.

ABOUT YORU FOUNDRY

Made for people who notice the details.

Yoru Foundry is built on a simple idea: everyday objects can carry intention, character, and artistry when they are designed and assembled with care.

We are starting with keyboards because they bring material, sound, touch, and function together in one object. Over time, the foundry is meant to grow beyond a single category while keeping the same standard of craft.
