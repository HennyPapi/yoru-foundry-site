THE PROCESS

Built around you, not a preset.

We start with how you want the board to feel, sound, look, and fit your daily use. From there, the parts and tuning follow.

01

Define the foundation

Layout, case material, mounting style, connectivity, and budget.

02

Shape the feel

Switch type, spring weight, stabilizer prep, and typing preference.

03

Tune the sound

Deep, balanced, crisp, or more expressive — tuned toward your preferred profile.

04

Finish the presentation

Keycaps, accents, desk aesthetic, and the final refined build identity.
