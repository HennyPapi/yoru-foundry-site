MATERIAL LIBRARY

Materials change the character of a build.

These cards will eventually use close-up photography and real sound samples.

Aluminum i

Clean, substantial and precise. Often chosen for structure, finish quality and a more direct response.

Polycarbonate i

Softer visually and mechanically, often associated with more flex and a less rigid response.

FR4 i

A useful middle ground for plate stiffness, resonance and cost.

Brass i

Dense, weighty and visually rich; useful when mass and a firmer response are desired.

PBT i

Textured, durable and commonly associated with a slightly denser keycap feel.

ABS i

Smooth, vivid and capable of a brighter or more resonant character depending on thickness and profile.
